Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026　Effective: November 20, 2026

Welcome to HeartDays (the “App”), provided by Xuanj (“we”). We take your privacy seriously. Please read this policy before using the App.

In short: the App has no accounts, runs no servers of its own, and contains no ads or third-party analytics. We do not collect, upload or sell your personal information. What you create stays on your own devices.

1. Information we handle

1. Content you create. This includes day names, dates, notes, icons, themes, category tags, reminder settings, the background images and collages you choose or make, and the name and birthday you enter on the welcome screen. It is stored in the App's storage on your device and shown in the App, widgets, Dynamic Island and Apple Watch. We cannot access it.

2. Records you write. You can add records to a day, made of text, photos and videos (picked from your library or captured with the camera). Photos are compressed to JPEG and videos to MP4 and stored inside the App; photos and videos you capture are not saved to the system Photos library. Deleting a record also deletes its photo and video files. We cannot access this content.

3. System data you authorize. Accessed only when you use the related feature and grant the system permission. You can revoke it any time in Settings:

  • Notifications: to deliver local reminders and milestone alerts at the times you choose. They are generated on your device, not by our servers.
  • Contacts: only when you tap “Import Contact Birthdays”, the App reads contact names and birthdays to create “X's birthday” days on your device. Contact data is not uploaded to us.
  • Calendar: only when you tap “Add to Calendar”, the App writes events to your Calendar. It uses write-only access and cannot read your existing events.
  • Reminders: only when you tap “Add to Reminders”, the App writes the day you chose to Reminders. iOS offers only a “full access” permission for Reminders, so the system prompt says the App wants to access Reminders, but the App only writes; it does not read or change your existing reminders.
  • Photos: when you pick a background, make a collage, or add photos and videos to a record, you choose them through the system photo picker. The App only receives what you select and stores it in the App.
  • Camera: used only when you tap “Camera” on a record, to take photos or record video.
  • Microphone: used only while you record a video, to capture its sound. The App never records in the background.

4. Device motion. The tilt parallax effect on home cards and the Star Sky reads the device's motion sensors (gyroscope). This needs no permission, and the data is used in real time on your device only; it is not stored, uploaded or used for anything else.

5. What we do not collect. We do not collect, on any server of ours, your name (the name you enter on the welcome screen stays on your device and is not synced), phone number, email, location, device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier), usage data or crash logs. We use no third-party analytics, advertising or push SDKs, and we do not track you.

2. Storage and sync

1. Local storage. Your data is stored only on your device by default. Deleting the App removes this data from the device, including the photos and videos in your records.

2. iCloud sync (optional, off by default). If you turn on iCloud Sync in Settings: the text of your days and records (names, dates, notes, reminder settings, record text, etc.) is synced using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, and the photos and videos in your records are synced between devices signed in to the same Apple ID through iCloud Drive, using your iCloud storage. This data lives in your iCloud account and is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we cannot access it. Background images and the name you enter on the welcome screen are not synced. You can turn sync off at any time. To delete cloud data, go to Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Account Storage and remove the App's data (including photos and videos in iCloud Drive). When you delete a record in the App, its photo and video files are also removed from the cloud.

3. Apple Watch. If you pair an Apple Watch, your list of days is transferred directly between your iPhone and watch using the system's WatchConnectivity, not through our servers.

4. Backup files. You can export a backup file (a .gooddays package) containing your days, record text, and the photos and videos in your records, and decide where to save or send it. Keep it safe: it contains your personal content.

3. Sharing and disclosure

1. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties.

2. Content you choose to share. When you use “Send to a friend” or “Share Card”, the day's name, date, notes, etc. are sent through the channel you choose to the person you choose. “Send to Friend (Link Only)” puts that content in the link itself; “Send to Friend (with Records & Media)” sends a package file that also contains the day's record text, photos and videos. Records are personal, so send them only to people you trust and avoid sharing anything you don't want others to see.

3. System features. Notifications, Live Activities (Dynamic Island / Lock Screen), Siri and Shortcuts, widgets and iCloud are provided by Apple's system services and are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

4. Legal requests. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot provide your personal content in response to a lawful request.

4. Your rights

You can view, edit and delete your days and records in the App; get a copy of your data (including photos and videos) with “Export Backup”; revoke Notifications, Contacts, Calendar, Reminders, Photos, Camera and Microphone permissions in system Settings; turn off iCloud Sync; and uninstall the App to remove all data on the device.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Minors should use the App under a guardian's guidance.

6. Security

Your data is kept in the App's sandbox on your device and protected by the system's device encryption and lock screen. iCloud sync uses Apple's transport and storage encryption. Please keep your device and Apple ID secure.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features or laws change. Updates will be shown in the App with a new “Last updated” date, and we will notify you prominently of material changes. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
